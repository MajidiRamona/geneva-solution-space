How to edit the website

Everything on the site can be changed from the editor at /admin/: the words on each page, the photos, the events, the partner list and the blog. No technical knowledge needed.

The one thing to remember

After you press Publish, the website takes about two minutes to update. Nothing looks different right away. Wait, then reload the page on the website. You don't need to press Publish twice.

Logging in

1

Go to /admin/ (the site address followed by /admin/).

2

First time? Open the link in your invitation email. It takes you to a page titled "Create your password" where you make up a new password for the editor (there is nothing to look up). After that, you land in the editor.

3

Every time after that: press Login and enter your email and the password you created.

4

Forgot the password? Press "Forgot password?" on the login screen; the email takes you to a "Choose a new password" page.

What's where

SectionWhat it changes
Blog postsArticles on the Blog page. Each post has a title, date, short summary, cover picture and the article itself.
EventsEvents on the homepage and the What We Do page. Future dates show under Upcoming; once the date has passed, the event moves to Past events by itself.
PartnersThe organizations on the spinning wheel on the homepage and on the Partners page: name, logo, description, website.
PagesThe words on every page, section by section. Also "Site settings" for the address, email and footer links.

Changing text on a page

1

Click Pages on the left, then the page you want (for example Who We Are).

2

Scroll to the section. Each section has a grey bar you can click to open or close it. The fields are labelled the way they appear on the page: "Heading", "Paragraph", "Button label".

3

Type your change. To make words bold or italic or to add a link, select the words and use the small toolbar that appears above the field.

4

Press Publish at the top, then Publish now. A green "Entry saved" message confirms it.

Headings come in two parts. "Heading" and "Heading (italic part)". The italic part is shown in the serif typeface in blue, like the titles on the site. Leave the italic part empty if you don't want one.

Writing a blog post

1

Click Blog posts, then + Blog post.

2

Fill in the title and the date. The summary is the one or two sentences shown on the Blog page under the title.

3

Add a cover image with Choose an image: pick a photo from your computer, or one already uploaded.

4

Write the article in the big "Body" box. The toolbar has bold, italic, headings, lists, links and a picture button. The right-hand side shows a preview.

5

Not finished? Switch on Keep as draft and press Publish: the post is saved but stays hidden from the website. Switch it off when it's ready.

6

Press Publish → Publish now. To remove a post later, open it and press Delete entry.

Adding an event

Click Events → + Event. Give it a title, a date, a short kind such as "Networking" or "Hackathon", and one or two sentences. The two nearest upcoming events appear on the homepage automatically.

Adding or changing a partner

Click Partners. To add one, press + Partner, upload a square logo, write a short description and the website. To remove one, open it and press Delete entry. The wheel on the homepage rearranges itself.

Photos

  • Anywhere you see Choose an image, you can upload from your computer or pick from the Media library at the top.
  • Photos straight from a phone are very large. If you can, resize to about 1600 pixels wide before uploading; the site will work either way, it just loads faster.
  • Partner logos look best square, on a white background.

If something goes wrong

  • I changed something and the site hasn't changed. Wait two minutes and reload. If it still hasn't, check that you pressed Publish → Publish now (the top bar should say "Published", not "Unsaved changes").
  • I made a mistake. Open the same item and correct it, then Publish again. Every change is also kept in a history, so nothing is ever truly lost. Ask whoever looks after the site to restore an older version if needed.
  • A field looks strange or the editor shows an error. Reload the page. If it keeps happening, take a screenshot and send it to whoever looks after the site.
