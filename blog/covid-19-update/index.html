Covid-19 Update:

We are back!

Following the latest Covid-19 protocols instituted by University of Geneva and EPFL, the SDG Solution Space has reopened for physical events at 1/3 capacity with strict compliance to Covid-19 safety measures. We look forward to welcoming you all our new and old partners to the innovation hub that grows our community. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact sdgsolutionspace@gmail.com.

We have missed you, see you soon in good health.

– The SDG Solution Space team.
