HackademIA 2026: Shaping the Future of Learning with AI

How can Artificial Intelligence improve the way we teach, learn and experience university life?

ShareLinkedInEmail
On this page 3 sections
  1. 18 challenges to explore
  2. From ideas to prototypes
  3. Join us for hackademIA 2026!

On Wednesday, 18 November 2026, the SDG Solution Space at the University of Geneva will host a new edition of hackademIA an evening hackathon dedicated to exploring innovative AI solutions for education.

Register here <https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLScgw29LoDd3TPeawqKdRkEB4OiMh1qQk17QwVmVZaFcsN-NLw/viewform>.

Note that due to limited space your  participation will be confirmed by the organizers.

Bringing together UNIGE students, faculty members and staff, the event offers an opportunity to turn ideas into practical solutions. Participants will work on real challenges related to teaching, learning, research and the student experience, with support from experts across academia, local tech companies, International Organizations and NGOs.

18 challenges to explore

This year's edition brings together 18 challenges proposed by students, university departments and external collaborators. Participants will have the opportunity to explore different ways AI can support education and university life.

Challenges from applicants

  1. Your Own Melody – Use AI to help students memorize material according to their psychological profile.
  2. Develop Critical Thinking – Develop learning methods in which AI strengthens rather than diminishes critical thinking.
  3. Catch the Flag – Organize hackathons to test UNIGE cybersecurity.
  4. The Library Benchmark – Continuously evaluate and score AI models and platforms for use by UNIGE students.

Challenges from previous hackademIA editions

  1. SmartFeedback – Help professors understand what students struggle with in real time.
  2. SmartSync – Develop AI-enhanced calendar syncing for UNIGE courses, exams and Moodle.
  3. MindMoodle – Create an AI-powered mental health support widget embedded in Moodle.
  4. HackCred – Use AI to evaluate hackathon participation for credit micro-certification.

GSEM and Cyberlab priorities

  1. AI Certification Course – Design a flexible course that keeps pace with the rapidly evolving expectations of employers.
  2. AI Hackathon Course – Imagine an AI-themed course involving several hackathons per semester, creating a three-credit course.
  3. AI Hackathons with Libraries – Collaborate with Geneva's public libraries to organise joint student hackathons.
  4. Open Innovation Space – Help design how a new innovation space, planned for 2028 in the SIP building, will be used.
  5. Open Source Economics – Imagine a course on Open Source Economics at GSEM, considering the growing role of open-source solutions in the AI sector.
  6. Open Source AI Hardware for Science – Develop a roadmap for students to create open-source hardware for AI-based research.

Challenges proposed by Open Science students

  1. AI Research Reading and Source Verification – Use AI to analyse scientific publications, generate summaries and research notes, verify citations and detect hallucinations.
  2. AI Negotiation Role-Play – Develop an AI role-play tool where students can practise UN-style negotiations and receive feedback, in collaboration with GIMUN.
  3. Accessible University Services Assistant – Develop an accessible AI assistant to help students navigate university platforms, courses and administrative procedures.
  4. Environmental Decision Simulator – Enable students to simulate environmental decisions and understand their consequences for energy consumption and CO₂ emissions.

From ideas to prototypes

HackademIA is more than an opportunity to discuss the potential of AI. It is a hands-on experience where participants collaborate to develop and test ideas.

Throughout the evening, participants will benefit from:

  • Practical AI challenges: Work on real issues affecting teaching, learning and student life.
  • Collaboration: Exchange ideas with UNIGE students, faculty members and staff.
  • Expert mentorship: Receive guidance from UNIGE professors, local tech companies, International Organizations and NGOs.
  • Prototyping and prizes: Develop prototypes and present innovative solutions.

The goal is to bring different perspectives together and explore how AI can contribute to a better university experience.

Join us for hackademIA 2026!

Whether you are interested in AI, education, research or innovation, hackademIA is an opportunity to experiment, collaborate and contribute to shaping the future of learning.

Event details

  • Date: Wednesday, 18 November 2026
  • Time: 16:00–22:00, followed by food and drinks
  • Location: SDG Solution Space, Campus Biotech Innovation Park, Avenue de Sécheron 15, Geneva
  • Registration: Scan the QR code on the event flyer to apply.

Shape the future of learning with AI. We look forward to seeing you there!
