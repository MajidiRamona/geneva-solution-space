The rapid evolution of Artificial Intelligence brings incredible potential—but for resource-constrained organizations and NGOs, knowing how to safely and practically experiment with AI can be a challenge.

That is why the SDG Solution Space is hosting IApéro, an afterwork networking event designed to bridge the gap between international organizations, academic talent, and local innovators.

What is IApéro?

It’s an informal gathering focused on real-world, responsible AI collaboration within the SDG ecosystem. You will get a firsthand look at how master’s students from UNIGE and EPFL, alongside local startups, are helping organizations test AI solutions ethically and practically.

The event will feature lightning pitches from student teams showcasing their recent collaborations with major partners, including:

  • The International Labour Organization (ILO)
  • TPG (Geneva Public Transport)
  • Data for Change & AICrowd

Following the presentations, we will open the floor for an informal Q&A and networking drinks. Whether you are looking to source university talent for a future project or just want to connect with the Geneva tech-for-good community, we would love to have you in the room.

Event Details

  • 📅 Date: Wednesday, July 1, 2026
  • 🕒 Time: 16:00 – 18:00
  • 📍 Venue: SDG Solution Space, Geneva
  • 🗣️ Language: Bilingual (English/French)

Spaces are limited, so make sure to reserve your spot ahead of time!
