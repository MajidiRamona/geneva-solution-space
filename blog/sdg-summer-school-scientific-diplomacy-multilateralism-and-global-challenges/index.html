SDG Summer School: Scientific Diplomacy, Multilateralism and Global Challenges

Are you ready to tackle the world’s most pressing challenges in the heart of International Geneva? The SDG Summer School 2026 is officially open for applications! This isn’t just a summer course—it’s a hands-on, high-imp…

ShareLinkedInEmail

This isn’t just a summer course—it’s a hands-on, high-impact workshop where you’ll collaborate with international experts to solve real-world problems.

🌐 The 2026 Mission: “Scientific Diplomacy, Multilateralism and Global Challenges”

This year, we are focusing on two critical frontiers:

  • Planetary Health: Analyzing the nexus of climate change and global health.
  • AI Governance: Setting the standards for the future of Artificial Intelligence.

🧬 Why You Should Apply:

  • Hands-on Learning: Move beyond the classroom and work in the SDG Solution Space.
  • Global Networking: Engage directly with representatives from International Organizations (IOs) and NGOs.
  • Earn Credits: Complete the program and earn 6 ECTS credits from the University of Geneva.
  • Diverse Backgrounds Welcome: Whether you’re a Bachelor’s or Master’s student, no prior technical knowledge is required—just your motivation to drive change.

🚨 THE CLOCK IS TICKING!

The application deadline is approaching fast. Don’t miss your chance to be part of the next generation of global problem solvers.

  • APPLICATION DEADLINE: March 2, 2026 (6 PM CET).
  • PROGRAM DATES: June 22 – July 3, 2026.
