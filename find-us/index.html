Find us

In the heart of International Geneva.

The Geneva Solution Space is hosted by the University of Geneva at Campus Biotech Innovation Park, in Sécheron on Geneva's Right Bank. We are a block from the World Meteorological Organization (WMO) and the World Trade Organization (WTO), and a 10-minute walk from the Palais des Nations, the UN's main campus in Geneva — with over 500 International Organizations, NGOs and diplomatic missions in the neighbourhood.

Where we are

Geneva Solution Space, University of Geneva
Campus Biotech Innovation Park
15 avenue de Sécheron, 1202 Geneva, Switzerland

The building is next to Campus Biotech, the joint health-sciences campus of the University of Geneva and EPFL.

Getting here

By train
About 10 minutes from Geneva Cornavin, the central station — take tram 15 towards Nations.
From the airport
About 15 minutes from Geneva Cointrin by taxi, or by train to Cornavin and then tram 15.
By tram
Take tram 15 to the “Maison de la Paix” stop. From the stop you will see a white building in an open-air car park — walk around the car park to the Campus Biotech Innovation Park entrance.
At the door
Use the intercom to have reception let you in, then ring the Solution Space from the electronic screen in the lobby.

Questions before you set off? Contact us.
