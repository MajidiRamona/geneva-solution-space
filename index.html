An innovation and education space for sustainability in the heart of International Geneva

The Geneva Solution Space, previously the SDG Solution Space, was established in 2017 at Campus Biotech Innovation Park to host the world's first comprehensive education programme on the Sustainable Development Goals.

What you can do here

A highly modular space for meetings and teamwork

The 330m² ground-floor space can be configured as a lecture room, a space for team-based project work, or a convivial meeting area. It also contains a 40m² kitchen, 60m² Fab Lab and 5m² Zoom Room.

Six formats we host most often.

01

Hackathons

Movable furniture, power everywhere, a kitchen and chill-out corner, a pitching area and prototyping tools in the Fab Lab. Example: hackademIA, a recurring student-driven hackathon with AI challenges set by International Organizations and NGOs, and the hackathons we host during the Open Geneva festival.

02

Workshops & trainings

University graduate courses, UN training sessions, high-school outings. The space and other rooms in the building can accommodate break-out activities. Facilitation partners available if you need help running your event.

03

Inspiration for innovation

The unique casual lab vibe, at the core of a building full of high-tech startups and globally active NGOs, contrasts with formal UN conference rooms, dull university auditoria and shiny corporate meeting spaces.

04

Product launches & demo pitches

Street-level entrance, space for 90 people standing, a beautiful rooftop terrace for the after-party, and a local audience of thousands of researchers and innovators from Campus Biotech.

05

Conferences & panels

Seats 60 facing a projection wall, with AV facilities for hybrid meetings and a convenient kitchen area for coffee breaks. Works for a 1–2 hour format or a multi-day conference.

06

Prototyping in the Fab Lab

The Fab Lab can be booked on its own, by the hour or the day, with partner AddictLab available to introduce your group to the machines.
