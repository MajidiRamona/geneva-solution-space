Partners

A guide for partners of the space.

What the Geneva Solution Space offers the organizations in its ecosystem, and how to use it: desks, meeting rooms, the ground-floor space, the Fab Lab and events.

Your desk on the 7th floor

The associated co-working space on the 7th floor of Campus Biotech Innovation Park hosts desks for up to 14 partner institutions, with 3 adjacent meeting rooms for video calls and small group discussions.

Desks are rented for periods of one year, renewable, and the Geneva Solution Space can serve as your Geneva address.

  • 560 CHF/ month
  • One yearrenewable
Enquire about desk availability

What partners can use

Meeting rooms
Three meeting rooms next to the 7th-floor desks, plus rooms across CBIP and the main Campus Biotech building — see how to book them below.
The ground-floor space
The 330m² space can be configured as a lecture room, a space for team-based project work, or a convivial meeting area — up to 60 people seated, 90 standing. Check availability and request a booking on the reservations page.
Kitchen & Zoom Room
A 40m² kitchen for coffee breaks and apéros, and a 5m² Zoom Room for hybrid meetings.
Fab Lab
The 60m² Fab Lab can be booked on its own, by the hour or the day, with partner AddictLab available to introduce your group to the machines.
Events & visibility
Host workshops, trainings, launches and panels in the space, and take part in the ecosystem's own formats — the IApéro evenings and the hackademIA hackathons, where partners set the challenges.
A place in the ecosystem
Partners appear on the ecosystem wheel on our homepage, alongside the University of Geneva and the International Organizations around Campus Biotech.

Meeting rooms at Campus Biotech and CBIP

CBIP 7th floor, Jura side
Three meeting rooms next to the partner desks — Mont-Blanc, Salève and Jura — bookable by ecosystem members. Bookings are made on the shared Google Sheet; ask the team for the link.
CBIP meeting rooms
Ground floor: Newton (16 people). 2nd floor: Vesta and Orion (16 people each, can be merged into one large room). 8th floor: Galilée (8 people). Book them on the Campus Biotech MyCampus platform.
Campus Biotech main building
Meeting rooms in the main building of Campus Biotech are booked on the same MyCampus platform — create an account first. biotech.pocketcampus.org ↗

Book the space

Bookings for the ground-floor space, the Fab Lab and the Zoom Room go through the reservations page: pick a date on the calendar and send a request. Meeting rooms are booked as described above.

The ecosystem

Current partners

The organizations listed below are partners of the Geneva Solution Space in 2026, most of them renting desks in our 7th floor co-working space. Together, they contribute to a vibrant ecosystem within the Campus Biotech Innovation Park.

If you are an international NGO, association or start-up working in the areas of human development and sustainability, and are interested to join, please contact us.

2050Today

A climate action initiative launched in October 2020 under the aegis of the Swiss Mission to the United Nations in Geneva. Over 60 institutions in International Geneva have committed to measure and reduce their emissions.

AddictLab Academy

An international creative think tank with dedicated research labs including the Chocolate Lab, Science Education Lab, Urban Green Lab, and Design Lab. Operating since 1997, they support the UN Sustainable Development Goals and develop SDG impact kits for educational and corporate workshops. Manages the Fab Lab in cooperation with the University of Geneva.

Visit addictlab.com ↗

Alliance for Health Promotion (A4HP)

An international NGO connecting civil society organizations to advance global health and well-being. Founded in 1997, collaborates with WHO and the UN to amplify community voices in health policy.

Visit alliance4healthpromotion.org ↗

Bridges to Development

Founded in 2018 by a group of global health experts who want to do development differently. Expertise in multilateral partnerships, strategy development, program implementation, and project management.

Visit bridgestodevelopment.org ↗

Citizen Science Global Partnership

A network-of-networks launched in December 2017 at the UN Science-Policy-Business Forum on the Environment, promoting citizen science for a sustainable world in partnership with UNEP and UNESCO.

Health Innovation Exchange (HIEX)

HIEX is a multilayered international platform supporting innovation ecosystems for climate resilience and improved access to healthcare.

Visit hiex.ch ↗

International Physicians for the Prevention of Nuclear War (IPPNW)

The only international medical organization dedicated to the abolition of nuclear weapons. Founded in 1980, awarded the 1985 Nobel Peace Prize. Launched ICAN in 2007.

Visit ippnw.org ↗

Open Geneva

A non-profit association that promotes and stimulates open innovation by bringing together a community of over 4,000 innovators. Organizes the Open Innovation Festival of Greater Geneva since 2017. Leads hackathon-support programming at the Geneva Solution Space.

Visit opengeneva.org ↗

Reos Partners

A global social impact company that helps people work together across differences to achieve systems change. Since their founding over 25 years ago, Reos Partners have worked on more than 43 projects in over 33 countries. Leads facilitation and process-support programming at the Geneva Solution Space.

Visit reospartners.com ↗
