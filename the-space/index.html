What you can host here

The space is used every week by University of Geneva classes, international organizations, NGOs and student groups. Some of the formats it works well for:

Classes & workshops

Master's courses, block seminars, trainings and hands-on workshops in small teams.

Hackathons

Day-long or weekend sprints, with room for teams to spread out and the Fab Lab next door.

Conferences & panels

Talks, book launches, side events and round tables, in person or hybrid.

Networking & apéros

Community evenings, launches and receptions, with the kitchen for catering.

Retreats & strategy days

Team retreats, planning sessions and board meetings away from the office.

Prototyping

Build and test ideas in the Fab Lab: laser cutting, 3D printing, CNC and vinyl.
